A Comprehensive Analysis of Horror Film Genres (Part A)

Explore the deepest fears within the human psyche and the inexplicable supernatural forces.

1. Psychological Horror

“The true fear always comes from within you.”

Uses the oppressive atmosphere, hallucinations, mental anxiety and unreliable narrators to create a continuous psychological pressure, leaving the audience unable to calm down for a long time after watching the film.

Classic Representative Work: The Shining

2. Supernatural Horror

“When gravity and modern science fail to explain everything...”

This is the most common and popular type of horror film. The core elements include evil spirits, ghosts, curses, demonic possession, and supernatural events. They often challenge human understanding of the real world, using unknown and supernatural forces to subvert the characters' rationality.

Classic Representative Work: The Conjuring
